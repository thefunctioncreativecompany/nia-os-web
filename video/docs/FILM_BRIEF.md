# Nia films: brief (v3)

The creative bible for the Nia launch film and every feature film. The agent (Claude Code)
reads this file in full before every task in `CODEX_PROMPTS.md`, studies the reference frames,
and checks its work against the **Quality bar** before reporting.

v3 (Ika, 2026-11-09): built from the actual Wix Harmony launch film, *Wix Harmony: The new way
to create* (~85s). Ika shared a screen recording, and 34 key frames plus 5 contact sheets
(one frame per second) are in `video/docs/reference/wix-harmony/`, named by timestamp. This
replaces v1 (Grok Bot style) and v2 (persona-led, which was based on a different film in Wix's series).

## 1. What the reference actually does

**One sentence:** on a soft, light canvas, the product builds four different businesses one
after another, each in a few seconds, through a mix of AI prompts and hands-on edits, and the
real UI is the star throughout.

### Why it works

1. **The UI is the hero, and it's real.** Real panels, real chips, real selection boxes; never
   a screenshot laid flat. Each piece is shown big and close.
2. **Prompt → result, again and again.** You see someone ask, the product think (shimmer), and
   the result land. Then a human hand-edits it. AI plus control is the whole message.
3. **Several different businesses.** Furniture, skincare, moto, outdoor: each with its own
   photography and display type, which proves range without saying it.
4. **Calm canvas, loud content.** The background is soft, near-white pastel. All the colour and
   attitude come from the content: photos, cut-outs and giant headline type.
5. **Very fast, but always readable.** About two visual changes per second (158 scene changes
   in 85s), but each moment shows one thing, centred, with plenty of space.
6. **A visible AI presence.** The orb gives the AI a character and tells you when it's working.
7. **Bookended:** a whisper of text, the logo, … the cluster floating out, the logo on white.
8. **Text on screen is sparse and quiet**: small, thin, lowercase pillar words. The big type is
   always *content*, never captions.
9. **Music-led.** No narrator or captions are visible. (Ika: confirm whether it has a voiceover.
   This brief assumes music and sound design only.)

### What we don't copy

Wix's mascot design, logo, product names, example businesses or exact layouts. We copy the
*principles* and the pacing.

## 3. Feature films: same language, shorter

Each feature film (Explore, Projects and templates, Schedule, Marketplace…) is 25–45 seconds:

1. Whisper: small text naming the feature ("explore", "call sheets")
2. Wordmark (1.5s)
3. The ask: one prompt or one action
4. Two or three productions using the feature, about 6–10 seconds each, prompt → result → hand-edit
5. A 3-second montage of the feature's smaller tools
6. Float out → wordmark on white

## 4. Look

- **Nia in light mode** (CLAUDE.md, binding): text `#1F1F1F`, neutral greys, **Nia Blue `#005FB8`
  as the only accent**, for active states, selection boxes and their labels, focus and the
  primary button. Geist, sentence case. No Obsidian, no Aged Gold. The AI draft marker exactly as
  the live app renders it.
- **Canvas** (film only; Ika approves before Step 4): near-white with a slowly moving mesh
  gradient of very pale tints: a cool blue from Nia Blue at about 6–10%, a soft grey-lilac and a
  hint of warm peach. Never saturated. No grain.
- **AI working** is shown as a soft, iridescent shimmer filling the area being drafted, which
  resolves into the result.
- **Copilot presence:** use whatever the live app uses for Copilot. If it has no avatar, propose
  two options to Ika (e.g. a glossy Nia Blue orb). Don't invent one without approval.
- **Content carries the colour:** Ika's photography, product and person cut-outs (transparent
  PNGs), and occasional 3D/chrome objects.
- **Selection boxes:** 2px Nia Blue frame, small corner handles, a filled Nia Blue label tab
  ("Call sheet", "Image"). Used everywhere something is chosen or edited.
- **Panels and chips:** white, 12–16px radius, soft large shadow, 1px light border.
- **Type:** Geist. Pillar and whisper words are small (40–56px at 1080p), Regular, lowercase or
  sentence case, dark navy-grey. Big type only as content (project titles, covers).
- **Cursor:** the standard arrow, visible whenever a human acts; quick, eased glides.

## 5. Motion language

- Everything in Remotion is driven by `useCurrentFrame()`. No framer-motion, CSS transitions or
  keyframes.
- **Pace:** about two visual changes per second, but one idea per moment, centred, with space.
- Panels and chips enter with gentle springs (scale 0.96 → 1, fade, 8–12px rise). No bounce.
- Camera: mostly 2D push-ins and pans with long eases; a 3D orbit only for the pillar ring and
  the float-out cluster.
- Typing: realistic rhythm (40–70ms per character, short pauses at spaces and punctuation), a
  blinking caret, then the send button pulses.
- Transitions: hard cuts, push-throughs (zoom into a panel until it becomes the next scene) and
  quick blur-whips (`@remotion/motion-blur`). No slides, wipes or cube effects.

## 6. Sound

- One upbeat, modern licensed track (Musicbed, Artlist or Epidemic Sound), about 115–125 BPM; cuts
  land on the beat. ElevenLabs music is fine for drafts; check its commercial terms before release.
- Sound design: soft UI ticks, typing, send whooshes, a shimmer sound when AI drafts, a saved tick
  on Apply, a lift into the float-out. Reuse `public/sounds/`.
- No voiceover unless Ika decides otherwise.
- Final mix −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. Keys only in `.env` / `.env.local`.

## 8. Architecture (in `video/`)

```
video/src/film/
  tokens.ts        Nia light-mode tokens (from src/app/globals.css)
  beats.ts         BPM, downbeat offset, beat()/bar() helpers
  canvas/          MeshGradient, Shimmer (AI working), SoftType (giant blurred type)
  primitives/      Camera, FloatRing (orbiting pieces), FloatCluster (3D float-out),
                   PushThrough, BlurWhip, Typewriter, SelectionBox, Chip, Panel, Cursor,
                   Whisper (small text), Wordmark
  ui/              frame-driven replicas of real Nia pieces in light mode: AppWindow, ProjectPage,
                   ProjectCard, CopilotPanel, CopilotPrompt, CopilotProposal, CallSheet, ShotList,
                   ScriptPage, TemplatePicker, CrewChip, ScheduleDay, LocationCard, ExploreTile,
                   TimelineStrip, PhoneFrame
  productions/     content for each example production (photos, names, crew, copy)
  films/           introducing-nia.tsx and one file per feature film, built on a shared
                   ShowcaseFilm structure (whisper → wordmark → ask → productions → montage → float out → wordmark)
  cutdowns/        Vertical, Square, Teaser
```

- UI replicas copy the real components' layout and tokens (`src/components/nia`, `src/components/ui`,
  `src/features`) as pure, frame-driven React, in **light mode**.
- The existing films and `Walkthrough.tsx` keep working until the new ones replace them.
- All `@remotion/*` packages are pinned to the installed Remotion version (4.0.538).

- [ ] Every moment shows one idea, centred, with space; about two visual changes per second.
- [ ] Prompt → AI shimmer → result → human edit appears in every production.
- [ ] No flat full-screen screenshot; UI is rebuilt, big and close.
- [ ] Nia's UI follows CLAUDE.md exactly (light mode, greys, Nia Blue only, Geist, sentence case).
- [ ] Canvas stays soft and pale; colour comes from content.
- [ ] Every feature shown is live in the app today.
- [ ] Only Ika's own imagery (or art-directed generated imagery); no client material without OK.
- [ ] No captions, no tutorial phrasing, no banned words.
- [ ] Cuts land on the music; the final wordmark holds at least 2.5 seconds.
- [ ] Renders without warnings; mix at −14 LUFS.
