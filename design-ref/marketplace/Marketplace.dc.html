Rental terms (summary)

  1. Rentals run per 24-hour day from pickup or delivery; a 1-day minimum applies.
  2. A refundable deposit is held before handover and released within 48 hours of an undamaged return.
  3. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before pickup; 50% of the rental after that; no refund once collected.
  4. Late returns are charged at 1.5× the daily rate for each day or part day.
  5. You're responsible for the item from handover to return. Damage protection reduces this to a $100 excess; theft and loss aren't covered.
  6. Items over $1,000 require ID verification, and the account holder must collect.
  7. Use the item lawfully and as intended; drones must be flown within local aviation rules by a qualified pilot.
  8. Nia facilitates the booking; the lister owns the item and is responsible for its condition and description.
